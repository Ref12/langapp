What changes, and what stays

E fits stroke bodies along their original slant. Reviewed source-point ranges remove brush entries. With Refine terminals on, a shared geometry pass shortens clear vertical-hook returns relative to their shafts and straightens short down-right strokes along their main bodies. Bounded endpoint insets account for round caps. No character-specific terminal coordinates are used. Turn it off to compare the previous E.

The rules leave long diagonals, steep stems, reversals, and compound enclosures alone. Hooks retain a return; dots retain a drawable centerline. Pen width changes the cap allowance, not the source artwork or stroke order. The blue overlay always shows A's untouched median guides.

Only the five displayed characters have reviewed entry-cleanup recipes. Writing uses their E paths at width 5.5 with terminal refinement enabled; the remaining 105 characters keep their source-median guides. Comparison controls do not change exercise settings. Bulk matches are review candidates, not a completed font conversion or proof of handwriting correctness. B and D appear only for tea.
