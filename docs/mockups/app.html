    Writing practice

    Start at the blue dot and follow the arrow.

    1. Full guide: trace the character with every stroke visible.
    2. One stroke at a time: finish each stroke to reveal the next.
    3. From memory: write without a model or upcoming stroke hints.

    Repeat each phase three times. Correct strokes snap into place in every phase; approximate strokes are welcome. Undo and Clear affect only the current repetition. Return to Dictionary to choose another character; your progress is kept until reload.

    Forgiving direction and shape checking, not handwriting recognition or a mastery score.
