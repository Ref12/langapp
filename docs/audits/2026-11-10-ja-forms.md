The generator now checks every conjugated token against what its headword builds and every other token written differently from its headword (`--check` prints them; a conjugated token that differs fails the check):

## は, へ, を that are not a particle token

Listed from every vocabulary reading and every phrase token of the pack that holds は, へ or を (src/data/packs/ja):

- Said wa, in `curriculum/japanese/authoring/romaji-overrides.json`: こんにちは (vocabulary), こんばんは (not in the data yet, listed so it never reads "konbanha"), ではありません (pattern name and token), はどうですか and はみんな (pattern names).
- A を is always o (rule, no entry needed): the words 授業を受ける, 電話をかける, the token をください, the patterns with を.
- A particle は or へ inside a pattern name is written wa or e because it stands alone between the separators (" + ", " / ", brackets, ？).
- Said ha or he, correctly spelled by the rule: はたらく, はい, ご飯, へや, はな, はち, はは, はん, はんぶん, はやい, はなす, 杯 (はい).
