## 3. What would be lost the day the old screens go

Lab (Chinese build) learners, **Japanese**, compared with `lab/ja/`:

- Their **progress stays in storage but is not visible or usable**: lessons marked as studied, words/grammar introduced, review cards and due dates for Japanese. The Japanese app starts empty (own DB). Small enough for step 4 if the owner wants it: a one-way copy (Q2). The id mapping is complete for words (291 of 291 entry ids of the lessons are in the pack's `band-1` vocabulary as `id` → `lb`) and nearly for grammar (115 of 117; two ids not in the pack), the 6 irregular form items (`ja:f:`) have no pack counterpart. FSRS card fields are the same.
- The **Chinese original under each phrase and the "with Chinese" lesson link**: gone by design (three separate apps). Not a step-4 gap.
- **Japanese words in the Dictionary** (search, levels, grammar notes, phrases, the Characters list with romaji search): the Japanese app has none; the old `OtherEntries` is the only place. Own step or one flag (Q3).
- **Forms table of a word**: no counterpart in the Japanese app (row 11); the Conjugate/form questions in Practice exist. Small: add the table to the lesson/word card's full view as `ja-forms.md` already planned; not a blocker for deletion but a loss.
- "Mark as studied" and "Next lesson" buttons: replaced by the pack flow; not a gap for most learners.
- Learners who start Japanese lessons **from Stable** are not affected (Stable keeps its screens).

**Korean** (no Korean app until step 5): everything above, plus **all** of Korean: lessons, practice, dictionary, Hangul writing guides, forms. Nothing of it can be closed inside step 4; it is step 5. This is why Q1 asks whether to keep Korean reachable.

Gaps small enough for step 4: the one-way progress copy (Q2, one stage), the Dictionary for ja if the area switch is enough (Q3, hypothesis to try first), the forms table on the Japanese word card (one component use). Gaps that are their own step: Korean (step 5), a real Japanese dictionary experience (search UI tuned for kana, N-levels as bands) if the flag shows it is not enough, the Chinese-original line.

## 4. Proposed stages

Each stage: all tests green, Chinese pins unchanged, publishable. Stages are in order; S1 needs no owner decision, S2 needs Q1 and Q2 answered, S3 needs Q3.

**S1: move what the pack-backed path and old rows need out of `core/parallel/`, no behaviour change.** Moves: `refs.ts` → `core/language/refs.ts` (`isLanguageRef`, `parseParallelRef`, `isParallelRef`, `parallelRef`, `parallelRefsOf`), `isParallelLessonId`/`parallelLessonId` out of `data.ts`, `romanize.ts` re-export removed (importers: `pinyin-context.ts`, `other-list.ts`, `parallel/*`), `parallel/conjugate.ts` re-exports removed (importers use `forms/conjugate`), `conjugate.test.ts` and `romanize.test.ts` moved next to the code they test. `ParallelLanguage` becomes a type alias of the two non-Chinese `LanguageId`s in `language/ids.ts`. Also: **rewrite `profiles/language-export.test.ts` and the `ParallelTracks` cases that prove row survival so they build their ja/ko rows as plain data** (and add one frozen old-backup fixture with ja:/ko: knowledge, cards, `ja:lesson:N`, `startedTracks`, journal events, a language `exerciseSessions` row), because those tests import `parallel/progress`/`practice`/`session` that later stages delete. Proves: every existing test passes with only import paths changed; the old-backup fixture restores and re-exports byte-identical in the Chinese build (and the Chinese golden export is unchanged). Must not touch: any screen, `package.json`, data, Chinese pins.

**S2: remove Japanese from the old screens and the switch's Japanese half; Korean untouched.** (Q1 answer: Korean stays until step 5.) Because the old screens are generic in `language`, "Japanese leaves, Korean stays" is mostly deleting `jaTrack`, the `ja` loader and the Japanese options (`parallelLanguages`, `DictLanguage`, `scriptsOf.ja`, switch glyph) rather than any screen: least code, no second set of screens. Includes the progress copy of Q2 if chosen (a "Bring my Japanese progress over" action that runs once in the Japanese app, same origin, reads the Lab learning DB: `ja:w:<id>`→`vocabulary:<lb>`, `ja:g:<id>`→`grammar:<lb>`, cards and journal as they are, lessons as the units they introduced). Stored ja rows stay in the Chinese DB untouched. Proves: Chinese build has no Japanese entry point (`#lessons/ja/1` is "not found", the picker lists Chinese and Korean), the Korean screens still pass their tests, old backup fixture still restores, the copy maps every id of the lessons. Must not touch: Korean data and screens, backup/restore code, Dictionary (S3).

**S3: Dictionary and "other language lines".** Depending on Q3: (a) switch `dictionary` on for `jaMeta` (test first whether `Dictionary.tsx` already works for the build's language: `languageInUse` returns it) and rename `OtherEntries`/`OtherCharacterCard` to neutral names, then delete the `ja` side of the Chinese Dictionary; or (b) delete `OtherEntries`, `OtherCharacterCard`, `other-list.ts`, `core/dictionary/{other,links}.ts` and the dictionary JSON for ja and accept the loss. Either way: remove `OtherLanguageLines`, the setting and its provider. Proves: ja Dictionary renders N5 word with reading and lesson link (a), Chinese Dictionary unchanged, `DictionaryLanguages.test.tsx` cases kept for ja or removed. Must not touch Korean pieces (kept until S5).

**S4: delete dead Japanese-only code and data.** `data/parallel/ja.json` and the ja half of `glosses.json`, `generate-app-parallel.mjs` ja output, `curriculum:check` and `parallel:generate` entries in `package.json` (owner edit: this is the only stage that changes it), `language?` on `BaseExercise`, `ParallelTilesExtra`, `taughtGrammar`/`inflectedTokens`/`buildFormPick` adapters, `planParallel` paths for ja, `data/tracks.ts` trimmed (jaTrack/koTrack names to `meta.ts`), `getPack().courseTracks`. Proves: `tsc -b`, the full suite, the packaged data check; sizes: bundle shrinks by the ja dictionary/lesson JSON. Must not touch `curriculum/`, scripts the pack generators use.

**S5: after step 5 (Korean is a pack): remove Korean the same way** (screens, `ko.json`, switch, `TrackHeader`, `ParallelPractice` etc., `scope.ts` simplified so `languageInUse` is the build's language, `currentLanguageOf` gone). This is where the last Parallel* files, the `LanguageSwitch`, and the `lessons`/`startedTracks` writers disappear. It must come after step 5's Korean app exists; if the owner chooses to drop Korean earlier (Q1 B), S5 merges into S2.

**Stored data and backup compatibility (all stages).** Rule: the code that *reads* an old row stays; the code that *writes* one goes. Concretely keep, in `core/language/refs.ts` (S1) and forever: `isLanguageRef`/`parseParallelRef` (used by `backup.ts`, `backup-codec.ts`, `backup/journal.ts`, `database.ts`, `profiles/vocabulary.ts`, `study/contracts.ts`, `study/knowledge.ts`, `assistant/tools.ts`, `scope.ts`, `Overview`), `isParallelLessonId`, the `currentLanguage`/`startedTracks`/`otherLanguageCards` fields in `model.ts` and the codec schema (`startedTracks` keeps the enum `['ja','ko']`), the journal decoder for language refs, the exports that add language rows to a Chinese backup, and the filters that keep them out of Chinese views and the AI context. Nothing deletes a row: no migration, no cleanup. A removal stage must run the frozen old-backup fixture of S1 through restore and export. Backups made in Stable or Lab before the removal restore after it; Stable keeps writing such rows, so this is not only history.
