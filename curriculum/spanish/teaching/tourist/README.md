# Spanish for a short visit

Start with `quick-start/sequence.yaml` for five self-contained units:
polite repair, food/drink, directions, payment questions, and asking for help.
No core course is assumed. Use `sequence.yaml` for all twelve units and the
additional transport, accommodation, shopping, scheduling, health/access,
lost-belonging, and changed-plan tasks.

The route contains 60 original phrases; the quick start contains its first 25.
Each unit introduces its own vocabulary and constructions before reviewing
them. The same precise sense IDs are used in the core, so later evidence can
remain useful without awarding an entire level or every meaning of a word.

Try recognition or pointing first, then change one detail and respond with
less support. Keep English available initially and hide it for retrieval.
Practice a few new senses at a time, revisit fragile material on later days,
and use a fresh situation rather than simply repeating a supplied answer.

## Readings and actual forms

`phrases.min.yaml` uses `{id, ch, pr, ds}`. Each `pr` is an **original,
wordwise broad IPA reading** using a pedagogical seseo/yeismo convention.
Spaces separate words; slash-delimited chunks are not a narrow transcription
of linked speech. Stress and lexical distinctions are retained, but this is
not a recording, source-verified phrase pronunciation, a promise of one
country's accent, or evidence of the learner's pronunciation.

Expanded phrase components preserve lexical and construction IDs plus
explicitly licensed surface/reading segments. Examples include irregular
present forms, `hay`, the contraction `al`, and infinitives with attached
clitics. These are usages of existing lemmas, not new headword counts.
Fixed grammar forms are assessed under their construction IDs.

Some polite forms can be learned as supported chunks before the learner
controls a complete verb paradigm. Using a memorized request does not prove
general command or subjunctive mastery. The route explicitly includes the
relevant prerequisites rather than depending on hidden core material.

## Scope and limitations

`Boleto` and `billete` have region- and context-dependent uses. Ask or confirm
local terminology; the route does not silently promise one pan-Hispanic
lexicon. Formal `usted`-style requests are a useful option, not a rule for
every relationship. Adapt wording to the situation.

These phrases are communication aids, not medical or legal advice, a safety
guarantee, or assurances that a payment method, dietary requirement, service,
facility, cancellation or refund will be available. State needs clearly and
confirm with appropriate local people; use qualified help for health/safety
concerns. Do not delay urgent assistance to complete a language exercise.

The phrase wording, translations and IPA are AI-authored and require human
linguistic review. Source vocabulary retains its attribution and limitations.
Text alone cannot assess listening or speech.
