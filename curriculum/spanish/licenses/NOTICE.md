# Spanish data attribution and reuse

The extracted/adapted lexical inventory and its derived compact vocabulary
views use English Wiktionary text under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Attribute **English Wiktionary contributors**, link the source entry URLs in the
expanded records, preserve this notice and `sources.yaml`, identify adaptations,
and follow the applicable attribution/share-alike requirements when
redistributing adapted data. No endorsement is implied.

The extraction is the Kaikki.org Spanish website-postprocessed snapshot of
2026-09-09, from the English Wiktionary dump of 2026-09-02. Credit **Kaikki.org,
Tatu Ylonen, and Wiktextract contributors**. English labels and teaching order
are adapted; the complete retained source gloss and reading distinguish the
original extraction from those adaptations. Local learning IDs are not
official Wiktionary, Cervantes, or examination identifiers.

The authoring candidate queue also used **FrequencyWords contributors,
Hermit Dave, and OpenSubtitles**, specifically the Spanish 2018 50k surface
frequency list at revision `525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6`.
That repository labels its **content CC BY-SA 4.0** and its **code MIT**.
No subtitle dialogue is redistributed.

The compact files are teaching data; `upstream` is only the small retained
build input and source lock. Quoted examples, audio, images, etymological
passages, auxiliary categories and merged semantic-link data are excluded.
Wiktionary can contain separately licensed quotations or media; the text
license is not a blanket license for those materials.

Original grammar explanations/examples, task descriptions, and travel phrases
are independently project-authored, with AI assistance and human-review
limitations. Consulting a lexical or pedagogical reference does not make every
original sentence a reproduction of that source. The dictionary notice does
not assert a blanket license for independent project authoring or override
other source rights. The RAE/ASALE and Cervantes links are reference citations,
not permissions to copy their dictionaries, inventories, textbooks, or exams.
