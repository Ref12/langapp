# Standalone French for a short visit

Start with `quick-start/sequence.yaml` for the first three ordered units, or
`sequence.yaml` for the complete route. Neither requires completion of core.
The prefix closes its own vocabulary, construction and review dependencies;
the remaining units extend it into transport, accommodation, food, health needs,
payment, disruptions and asking for help.

These are original practical formulae, not copied phrasebook passages or promises
about regulations, telephone numbers, treatment, services or local availability.
Ask for clarification, explain a need and confirm understanding. Do not infer a
medical diagnosis or guaranteed response from knowing a phrase.

Phrase `pr` is a **segmented reading guide**. Slash-delimited source lexical
readings may appear beside separately identified original contextual chunks.
Spaces between chunks show the teaching segmentation, not mandatory pauses.
This is not a continuous narrow transcription or an observed audio model.
Elision, contractions, article choices and relevant contextual effects are
licensed by explicit French frames rather than a universal pronunciation rule.
Slots require canonical forms by default; a particular plural or feminine
realization must be explicitly licensed. Sharing a lexical ID is not sufficient
to make a different gender or number agree in the sentence.
The coverage is France-centered; it does not claim to teach all regional
varieties or every speaker's realization.

`phrase_components` separates lexical evidence from construction evidence and
retains full surface realizations. Fixed expressions can be construction-only:
learning a polite request does not automatically award an unrelated dictionary
sense or the whole conditional paradigm. Source-attested spelling/inflection
realizations and original formula pronunciation are distinguished in
`../../realization-provenance.yaml`. All original formulae still need human
linguistic review.
