# Teaching practical French

Use the [program goals and checkpoints](program.yaml) as destinations, not as a
calendar or promise of a proficiency label. The six phases cover levels 1-4,
5-8, 9-13, 14-18, 19-24 and 25-30. Their directories have numbered prefixes;
globally numbered levels live within each phase's `levels/` directory.

The authored themes support adaptive small lessons. A generated module groups
related material, but is not a demand to introduce its whole inventory at once:
usually select about 5-8 new senses, combine them with a meaningful construction,
then change the situation and retrieve earlier material. Return to fragile items
after a delay. Later phases have genuinely broader inventories and more demanding
explanations, synthesis, audience adaptation and interpretation tasks.

The learner-facing projections use exactly `{id, ch, pr, ds}` for vocabulary and
phrases, and `{id, ch, ds}` for grammar. `ch` means the French written form; `pr`
is a documented reading guide; `ds` is a concise English disambiguator of at most
64 characters. Introductions **and reviews** repeat the complete canonical
compact record. Expanded source data do not leak into these compact mappings.

Follow [mastery.yaml](mastery.yaml): recognize, understand, supported-use and
independent-use are per-item stages. Reading, listening, typed production,
spoken production and handwriting have distinct evidence. A tutor may select a
route based on an observed need, but must not fabricate learner history, infer
mastery from a source band, or count reading IPA as listening performance.

[Grammar notes](grammar-notes.md) and [pronunciation policy](pronunciation.md)
explain French-specific contrasts and limits. In particular, teaching one verb
sense does not award every conjugation; identifying a fixed polite formula does
not award unrelated lexical senses of its components. Accents, elision,
contractions and grammatical gender must not be discarded during matching.

Professional and technical branches are optional after level 18; scientific
and literary branches are optional after level 24. A branch may explicitly
introduce a sense that appears later in core, using the **same** identity and
compact record. Already-known selections are reviews, not duplicate new breadth.
No branch supplies occupational certification.

The [tourist route](tourist/README.md) is independent of core. Its quick-start
prefix begins with no assumed vocabulary or grammar prerequisites and teaches
narrow, useful formulae before broader practical situations.
