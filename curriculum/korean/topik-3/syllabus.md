# Stage 3 — explanations, advice and familiar social life

**Version:** 2026-09-07. **Exam orientation:** TOPIK II, award level 3.
**Educational reference:** NIKL Standard Curriculum 2020-54 level 3,
paraphrased: familiar social exchanges, advice, recommendations, explanations
and information sharing. TOPIK II includes listening, reading and writing;
speaking remains an additional teaching domain here, not an ordinary TOPIK II
section. Sources and license distinctions are in `../sources.yaml`.

## Prerequisites

Stage 2 outcomes: connected routine accounts, permissions and obligations,
basic clause modification, formality control and Hangul fluency. Diagnose
with a service complaint, a plan-change audio task and a short connected
message. Verify number/tense precision before introducing quoted information.

## Communicative outcomes

- **Listening:** follow accounts and advice, distinguish a quoted statement
  from a question/instruction and track a cause versus a sequence.
- **Speaking:** recount experience, report another person's message, make and
  justify a recommendation, and explain unexpected events.
- **Reading:** follow familiar social texts, instructions and short articles;
  locate the writer's main point and its supporting details.
- **Writing:** organize an account or practical explanation into linked
  paragraphs with an identifiable topic, sequence and reason.
- **Pragmatics/phonology:** distinguish one's observation, inference and
  hearsay; maintain respectful and humble forms across embedded clauses.

## Practice, review and sound

Use message relay, incident reconstruction, two-person advice exchanges,
process diagrams, small-group planning and 2-paragraph informational writing.
Give original model texts with English versions; require learners to mark
whose claim each statement is before paraphrasing it. Contrast genuine passive
verbs with simply adding a suffix to any verb.

Apply ID-based spaced retrieval at 1/3/7/14/30 days, varying the example and
speaker relationship. Every three blocks mix prior-stage transactions with new
reporting, inference and sequencing tasks. Revisit nasal/liquid assimilation,
consonant tensing, final clusters and sentence grouping; assess whether the
listener can recover the message, not whether speech matches one accent.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

| Skill | Task | Evidence for score 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Two unfamiliar 60–90-second everyday/social accounts | Recover speaker roles, reported content, causal sequence and proposed response; at least 8/10 details correct without upgrading guesses to facts |
| Speaking | A 4-minute account followed by advice and clarification | Relate an experience, report a message accurately, justify advice and respond to two follow-ups; recover from a misunderstanding without an English rescue |
| Reading | Two social/informational texts totaling about 700–1,000 Korean characters | State the main point and evidence, identify time/causal relationships and distinguish author assertion from reported opinion |
| Writing | A 300–450-character practical explanation or account | Produce a clear two-paragraph progression with an example, a reason and a recommendation; roles and tense remain recoverable |

Apply the common five-dimensional 0–4 rubric. At this stage, form control
especially means quotation morphology and role/voice accuracy; organization
means connected explanation beyond sentence chaining; pragmatics includes not
using a shared-knowledge ending as an unjustified reprimand. Score 4 transfers
the explanation to a new audience and handles an unexpected follow-up. Require
3 in every dimension on each skill task and on a parallel delayed task; target
any recurrent role, source or inference confusion before progression.
