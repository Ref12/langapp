# Korean writing source-gap assessment

The source assessment provides inventory and upstream feasibility evidence,
**not imported Hangul handwriting geometry**. The subsequently authorized
original, unreviewed pilot has its own source identity and terms in
`ORIGINAL-WRITING-PILOT.md`. Ordinary system-font labels in its preview identify
Unicode scalars; the displayed candidate strokes come only from the separately
identified original recipes.
