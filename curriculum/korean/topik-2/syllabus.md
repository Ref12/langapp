# Stage 2 — routine public situations and connected accounts

**Version:** 2026-09-07. **Exam orientation:** TOPIK I, award level 2.
**Educational reference:** NIKL Standard Curriculum 2020-54, Table 2 level 2,
paraphrased: routine public exchanges, information requests, permission and
necessary everyday messages. This original teaching rubric is not an official
TOPIK marking scale. See the reference and original-content records in
`../sources.json`.

## Prerequisites and diagnostic

Stage 1 outcomes or equivalent: decode Hangul without a romanization scaffold,
use polite basic clauses, track subject/object/location, distinguish past and
future, interpret everyday numbers and request clarification. Diagnose with a
new service role-play and a six-sentence account. Revisit final consonants,
linking and contractions before increasing speech speed.

## Communicative outcomes

- **Listening:** follow routine explanations and requests at clinics, shops,
  transport facilities and classes; recognize permissions and restrictions.
- **Speaking:** explain a minor problem, ask for a service, give a reason,
  negotiate an alternative and describe plans or experience.
- **Reading:** understand short notices, instructions and messages organized
  into connected sentences, including sequence and comparison.
- **Writing:** create a coherent personal account or request with time,
  description, condition and reason.
- **Pragmatics:** distinguish formal service talk, conversational politeness,
  subject honorifics and humble actions benefiting others.

## Practice and review

Build appointment changes, missing-item descriptions, a clinic visit, a room
comparison and a travel itinerary. For each pattern, compare both translated
examples, complete a meaningful choice task, then transfer to speaking/writing.
Ask learners to distinguish a refusal from inability, a personal wish from a
third person's desire, and subjective impression from fact.

Use 8–15 new active entries per session and spaced retrieval at about
1/3/7/14/30 days. Every third block includes earlier-stage transaction, listening
and writing tasks. Add contrast sets for regular/irregular predicate inflection,
nasal assimilation, aspiration and tensing using teacher-checked pronunciations.
Assess speech in chunks, not as isolated perfect syllables. All tutor Korean
models and feedback must have an English counterpart available.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

| Skill | Task | Evidence for score 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | A service explanation and a plan-change conversation | Identify main purpose, the restriction/permission, reason and next action; answer at least 8/10 factual or sequence questions |
| Speaking | A 3-minute service problem and negotiated alternative | State the problem, request help, answer follow-up questions, compare two options and confirm the agreed plan using appropriate politeness |
| Reading | Two notices plus a short personal account, about 350–500 Korean characters total | Retrieve at least 8/10 details and infer what the reader should do; distinguish completed and planned actions |
| Writing | A connected 150–250-character message or account | Include purpose, relevant context, reason, request/plan and closing; use relative clauses and connectors without obscuring the message |

Use five dimensions and the 0–4 anchors in `../README.md`. For organization,
look for a connected paragraph rather than a list. For social appropriateness,
look for a suitably softened request and consistent treatment of an elder or
service worker, not merely a formal ending. Score 4 requires transferring the
task to an unexpected change while preserving meaning and register. Each task
must reach 3 in every dimension and succeed again one week later. Remediate the
specific relation—time, condition, role or inference—rather than adding more
words without correcting the underlying misunderstanding.
