# Stage 1 — first independent everyday exchanges

**Version:** 2026-09-07. **Exam orientation:** TOPIK I, award level 1.
**Educational reference:** NIKL Standard Curriculum 2020-54, Table 2 level 1,
paraphrased: brief basic exchanges, introductions, familiar objects and simple
messages. This is an original four-skill curriculum, not an official TOPIK
syllabus or scoring rubric. See `../sources.yaml`: `nikl-standard-2020`,
`niied-topik`, `sejong-curriculum`, `original-ko`.

## Prerequisites and entry check

No prior Korean conversation is required. Complete the Hangul introduction
before the sentence blocks: recognize and compose syllable blocks, distinguish
basic vowels and plain/aspirated/tense consonants, identify final consonants,
type a short word, and match a heard syllable to its spelling. Use the translated
phonology examples in `../README.md`. Check hearing/accessibility needs and
avoid using romanization as the sole route to pronunciation.

## Communicative outcomes

- **Listening:** identify names, numbers, times, prices and destinations in slow
  short exchanges; request repetition rather than guess.
- **Speaking:** introduce a fictional self, state location/preferences, buy an
  item, order a meal and arrange a simple meeting using polite endings.
- **Reading:** extract explicit information from signs, menus, timetables and
  short messages; distinguish who does what to whom.
- **Writing:** enter basic information and write linked short sentences about
  identity, routines, yesterday and tomorrow.
- **Pragmatics/phonology:** select polite rather than familiar speech with
  strangers; distinguish listener politeness from honoring a subject; produce
  intelligible syllable-final contrasts and elementary linking.

semantic heuristic. Each block expands into multiple sessions as needed.

## Practice and cumulative review

Use picture choice, listen-and-point, object/location information gaps, a price
and quantity exchange, calendar scheduling, and a two-message conversation.
Start with both translated examples for each pattern, then change one slot and
finally the communicative situation. Use original prompts with English keys.
Introduce roughly 8–15 active words per session, plus controlled receptive
support. Review after 1/3/7/14/30 days, adjusting after errors.

After blocks 3, 6 and 9, run a new five-minute transaction and a short message
task covering earlier grammar. Keep an ID-based mastery ledger, not a raw
word-count target. Repeat sound discrimination, linking, native/Sino-Korean
number selection, vowel contractions and spacing throughout. Correct one
high-impact error at a time; do not interrupt every hesitant syllable.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

These are local teaching tasks; TOPIK I itself does not assess this speaking
and writing portfolio.

| Skill | Task | Evidence for score 3 (independent, minor nonblocking errors) |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Two unseen short transactions, each replayed at most twice | Correctly record at least 8 of 10 requested facts, including quantity, time and destination; identify a negated fact |
| Speaking | 60–90-second introduction, then a 6-turn order or meeting arrangement | Complete identity, preference, quantity and time functions; ask for clarification once when needed; understandable without the tutor supplying sentences |
| Reading | A menu, timetable and three messages, about 150–250 Korean characters in total | Locate at least 8 of 10 facts; distinguish action location from destination and identify one request/prohibition |
| Writing | Six to eight original sentences about self, routine, yesterday and tomorrow | Required information is clear; basic particles and time endings distinguish roles/time; spacing and spelling do not prevent comprehension |

Score each task on fulfillment, meaning/comprehension, form, organization/fluency,
and social appropriateness using the 0–4 anchors in `../README.md`. In this
stage, organization means an intelligible sentence sequence; register means
maintaining polite speech in the service interaction. A 4 additionally handles
a changed price, time or identity without memorized answers. Require at least
3 on every dimension for every skill, and confirm on a parallel task one week
later. If below threshold, reteach the failed function with fewer lexical
choices; do not simply repeat the identical item until memorized.
