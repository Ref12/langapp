# Grammar coverage and dictionary gaps

Grammar constructions are independently addressable learning items. A missing
dictionary sense must not cause an important available construction to disappear
from the curriculum, nor justify assigning an unrelated word sense to it.

In particular, the pinned vocabulary does not supply a suitable disposal-marker
sense for the word used in `zh-hsk3-g001`. That construction is nevertheless
present in the original grammar inventory and is taught at level 7. Its
resulting-location and categorization uses are taught under `zh-hsk4-g009`
and `zh-hsk4-g011`. Do not award mastery of a grasping, handle, or classifier
sense merely because the learner correctly uses the disposal construction.

## Constructions that introduce their own fixed forms

The following existing source grammar IDs also cover fixed expressions for
which the dictionary lacks a suitable addressable sense or chunk. Learn the
fixed form as part of the construction, using the original grammar note and
bilingual examples. The compact construction and its English disambiguator
identify the learning item without fabricating a dictionary ID.

| IDs | Function |
| --- | --- |
| `zh-hsk3-g001`, `zh-hsk4-g009`, `zh-hsk4-g011` | Affect a known object; specify its resulting location; treat it as something |
| `zh-hsk5-g015`, `zh-hsk6-g017` | Restrict an evaluation to a perspective or dimension |
| `zh-hsk6-g001` | Reason from one case to an even stronger case |
| `zh-hsk6-g011` | Open a possibility through cautious double negation |
| `zh-hsk6-g022` | Enumerate two reasons for the same decision |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g008`, `zh-hsk7-9-g009` | Compare causal attributions; acknowledge and answer a counterargument |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g012`, `zh-hsk7-9-g024` | Optional literary counterfactual and universal-affirmation forms |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g013`, `zh-hsk7-9-g016` | Formal universal scope and relationships between concepts |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g023` | A substantial quantity, not a claim about every member |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g025` | Highlight a rare or difficult positive quality |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g026`, `zh-hsk7-9-g027` | Rhetorical certainty or obviousness, not automatic evidence of truth |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g028`, `zh-hsk7-9-g029` | Provisional assumptions and explicitly restricted arguments |

`anchors` in the authored grammar table lists available lexical sense
prerequisites; it is not an exhaustive segmentation of the construction.
An empty list can mean that the fixed form is introduced by the grammar item
itself. Any other example vocabulary must already be known or be explicitly
glossed. Assess the construction under its grammar ID, without inferring
independent vocabulary knowledge for its components.

## Variants not made separate requirements

These source variants remain reference material. Their central function is
already taught; the tutor can present them as explicitly glossed variants
without inventing additional required mastery distinctions.

| Source variant | Closely related selected construction |
| --- | --- |
| `zh-hsk4-g007` | `zh-hsk3-g009`: compatible qualities or activities |
| `zh-hsk5-g005` | `zh-hsk3-g008`: covarying degree, with added emphasis |
| `zh-hsk6-g021` | `zh-hsk5-g004`: an outcome reverses an expectation |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g007` | `zh-hsk4-g006`: replace an incorrect characterization |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g011` | `zh-hsk6-g005`: formal hypothetical condition |
| `zh-hsk7-9-g014` | `zh-hsk3-g011`: a necessary condition |

Some source word glosses are broader than their contextual grammatical use:
`zh-hsk1-00239-s005`, `zh-hsk3-00753-s002`, `zh-hsk1-00207-s007`,
`zh-hsk3-00867-s005`, and `zh-hsk7-9-05182-s001`. Use the selected construction's
note and examples to establish the intended function rather than treating
the isolated English hint as a complete explanation.

The source levels and IDs remain unchanged. These notes and placements are
authored teaching judgments, not an official grammar mapping or linguistic
certification.
