# Band 3: connected everyday accounts and problem solving

**Basis:** GF0025-2021 educational framework; 953 incremental source headwords,
2,209 cumulative, and 25 original teaching constructs. Not the 2025/2026 exam
syllabus. Pattern IDs are `zh-hsk3-gNNN`.

## Prerequisites and outcomes

Demonstrate bands 1–2 routines, basic aspect, comparisons and practical requests.
Check comprehension of result versus potential complements and definite versus
new objects before introducing affected-object constructions.

The learner can explain a practical mishap, describe changing habits, give
conditional advice, compare experiences and sustain a connected conversation.
Use clear pronunciation and comprehensible paragraph organization; these are
general performance goals, not a certificate equivalence.

## Ordered recurring lesson blocks

Introduce 10–14 useful new entries per session only when review remains
manageable. Search English glosses as well as heuristic topic labels and record
the exact selected IDs. Recycle earlier vocabulary in every block.

| Order | Theme and practical output | Vocabulary retrieval | Grammar IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resolve a lost or broken object; describe a scene | `health-and-body`, `travel-and-places`; break, lose, hold, understand | `zh-hsk3-g001`–`zh-hsk3-g005` |
| 2 | Describe habits, trends and added responsibilities | `study-and-work`, `thought-and-communication`; continue, improve, interest | `zh-hsk3-g006`–`zh-hsk3-g010` |
| 3 | Give rules and report how an event happened | `society-and-public-life`; condition, member, immediately, arrive | `zh-hsk3-g011`–`zh-hsk3-g015` |
| 4 | Offer advice and choose alternatives | `health-and-body`, `people-and-relationships`; rest, choose, need, allow | `zh-hsk3-g016`–`zh-hsk3-g020` |
| 5 | Discuss attitudes, similarities and learning history | `study-and-work`, `time-and-quantity`; experience, duration, repeat, same | `zh-hsk3-g021`–`zh-hsk3-g025` |

Run a remaining-ID audit after every thematic cycle. Allocate every still-unseen
row, including general/abstract entries, to a repeated block; full inventory
coverage must not depend on finding a matching keyword.

## Practice and review

Alternate short bilingual narratives, scene reconstruction, picture or text
information gaps, correction of ambiguous reference, and two-person problem
solving. Practice necessary versus sufficient conditions and distinguish
potential ability from the quality of an actual performance. Require a new
context after a successful substitution, not only repetition of the example.

Use next-day, three-day, weekly and monthly spaced checks; bring unstable
aspect/complement pairs back sooner. Every fifth session integrates three
modules. Keep listening, speaking, reading, typing and handwriting evidence
separate. The supplied files contain no audio: do not score pronunciation from
text or claim a synthesized voice is an official recording.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

1. Narrate a three-minute practical problem with cause, affected object, outcome
   and proposed remedy; answer five follow-ups with at most one content prompt.
2. Work through an eight-turn plan negotiation involving a necessary condition,
   a sufficient condition and one changed preference; correctly distinguish the
   two conditional relationships.
3. Read a 250–400-character original passage, identify the main point and at
   least 80% of key details; write 150–200 characters connecting two experiences.
   Use accessible English equivalents in materials and post-task feedback.

Score five dimensions 0–4: **completion and cohesion**, **comprehension**,
**lexical/aspect/complement control**, **intelligibility and script accuracy**,
**interaction and self-repair**. A 4 meets all task conditions independently;
3 achieves the main communicative purpose with minor nonblocking errors and
one clarification; 2 requires repeated support or loses a major relationship;
1 offers only disconnected fragments; 0 provides no relevant evidence.
Suggested exit is 15/20, each dimension at least 2, on two different tasks.

Audit exposure to all 953 new IDs and test 100 new plus 25 earlier IDs, balanced
across themes and recognition/production: aim for 85% recognition and 75%
appropriate contextual production. Review weak categories before promotion.
These are tutor-defined thresholds; use separately licensed, current official
materials for actual examination-format preparation.
