# Band 1: personal life and first interactions

**Basis:** GF0025-2021 educational framework; 506 incremental normalized source
headwords, 25 original teaching constructs. Not the 2025/2026 exam syllabus.
Grammar IDs below use `zh-hsk1-gNNN`. No official grammar mapping is asserted.

## Prerequisites and outcomes

No prior Mandarin is required. Begin with syllable initials/finals, four lexical
tones, neutral tone, syllable boundaries and pinyin input. Introduce simplified
character recognition and basic stroke direction alongside words; a complete
character inventory is not supplied. Assess hearing or speech only from audio.

The learner can introduce people, identify possessions, ask simple questions,
buy a few items, locate familiar places and state a daily plan. Short memorized
forms should develop into small substitutions and independent requests.

## Ordered recurring lesson blocks

Each block repeats across multiple sessions. Introduce about 6–10 new entries
per session after reviewing due material, reducing the load when recall drops.
Use current-level vocabulary topics and case-insensitive English-gloss searches, plus
all earlier taught items. Record the exact IDs selected.

| Order | Theme and task | Vocabulary retrieval | Grammar IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet a classmate; identify people and possessions | `people-and-relationships`; student, teacher, name, family, have | `zh-hsk1-g001`–`zh-hsk1-g005` |
| 2 | Ask and answer basic information questions | who, what, where, whose; `time-and-quantity` for counted objects | `zh-hsk1-g006`–`zh-hsk1-g010` |
| 3 | Locate objects and plan a day | `travel-and-places`, `study-and-work`; home, school, today, tomorrow | `zh-hsk1-g011`–`zh-hsk1-g015` |
| 4 | Explain shared preferences and beginner abilities | `thought-and-communication`; like, study, drink, swim | `zh-hsk1-g016`–`zh-hsk1-g020` |
| 5 | Request help; buy items; follow simple directions | `food-and-shopping`; price, water, book, come, go; prohibition forms | `zh-hsk1-g021`–`zh-hsk1-g025` |

After each thematic pass, assign remaining unseen IDs, including
`general-and-abstract`, to a suitable block. Repeat until all 506 are introduced.
Do not replace the full inventory with the search results alone.

## Practice and review

Use meaning-to-sound and sound-to-meaning retrieval, tone discrimination with
reviewed audio, classifier matching, substitutions and a three-turn dialogue.
Show each target utterance with its English equivalent during modeling; hide
English only for the retrieval turn, then reveal it. Contrast identity versus
adjective predicates, possession versus location, and question types.

Review after the session, next day, three days, one week and one month; shorten
the interval after an error. Every fifth session mixes earlier modules. Keep
separate recognition, spoken-production and written-recognition evidence.
No native-like accent requirement; prioritize distinguishing meaning.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

Use unfamiliar combinations of taught content, not memorized example recitation:

1. A 90-second introduction including identity, family, location and preferences;
   answer four follow-up questions, with one repetition request permitted.
2. A shop role-play with quantities, prices and a polite request; communicate
   at least four of five requested facts correctly.
3. Read five short messages and select the correct action in four cases.
   Write or type six meaningful personal sentences; handwriting is separately
   observed and not inferred from typing.

Score each dimension 0–4: **task completion**, **comprehension/response**,
**word and grammar control**, **sound/script intelligibility**, **interaction
and repair**. Here 4 = independently meets every task condition with only minor
slips; 3 = meets the main conditions with at most one prompt and errors that do
not block meaning; 2 = communicates about half with repeated prompts; 1 = isolated
memorized words only; 0 = no relevant evidence. Suggested teaching exit:
at least 15/20 with no dimension below 2, on two different occasions.

Additionally audit that every inventory row has been introduced and sample
50 stratified IDs: at least 80% meaning recognition and 70% supported production
in an appropriate phrase. Re-teach failed clusters rather than hiding them in
an average. These are local teaching thresholds, **not an HSK pass score**.
Schedule any current exam-format familiarization only from the official exam
materials appropriate to the learner's actual test.
