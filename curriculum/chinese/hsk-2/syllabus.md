# Band 2: routines, experiences and practical choices

**Basis:** GF0025-2021 educational framework; 750 new normalized headwords
(1,256 cumulative), 25 original constructs. Not the 2025/2026 examination list.
IDs use `zh-hsk2-gNNN`; placements are authored teaching decisions.

## Prerequisites and outcomes

Complete band 1 or demonstrate its exit tasks. Recognize basic pinyin and tones,
simple clauses, common classifiers, questions and location phrases. Diagnose
new-situation versus completed-event meanings before teaching more aspect.

The learner can narrate a short everyday event, describe current activity,
compare two practical options, explain a simple reason, arrange a trip and
request permission or help. Speaking and writing develop beyond test-item
recognition; no exam score is inferred from vocabulary recall.

## Ordered recurring lesson blocks

Use 8–12 new words per session when due-item recall is stable. Select CSV IDs by
the listed topics plus English-gloss keyword searches; retrieve prior-band
prerequisites first. The blocks recur until the whole new inventory is taught.

| Order | Theme and task | Vocabulary retrieval | Grammar IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Describe yesterday, a change and an experience | `time-and-quantity`; yesterday, finish, arrive, experience | `zh-hsk2-g001`–`zh-hsk2-g005` |
| 2 | Explain what is unfinished; plan a sequence | `study-and-work`, `food-and-shopping`; prepare, cook, wait | `zh-hsk2-g006`–`zh-hsk2-g010` |
| 3 | Choose between weather, travel or purchase options | `travel-and-places`, `nature-and-technology`; cold, far, price | `zh-hsk2-g011`–`zh-hsk2-g015` |
| 4 | Give feedback and report completed results | `thought-and-communication`; slow, clear, finish, obtain | `zh-hsk2-g016`–`zh-hsk2-g020` |
| 5 | Find a route; arrange times; ask permission | `travel-and-places`, `time-and-quantity`; station, distance, telephone | `zh-hsk2-g021`–`zh-hsk2-g025` |

Track all 750 new IDs, including general/abstract and unmatched entries.
At the end of each pass, assign the remaining unseen IDs to recurring themes.
Do not exclude entries merely because a heuristic topic is unhelpful.

## Practice and review

For each construct: model both bilingual examples, contrast an easily confused
form, elicit a controlled change, then use it in an information-gap task.
Distinguish completed action, experience, ongoing action and a changed state;
English past tense alone is not a selection rule. Practice route listening with
reviewed speech, then orally reconstruct it. Type a daily log and correct it.

Review at one, three, seven, fourteen and thirty days, with same-session repair
after mistakes. Every fifth session includes a band-1 comparison task and mixed
dictation of already reviewed words. Count a recalled word as productive only
when the learner selects the relevant sense in a comprehensible new phrase.
All new tutor-authored Chinese must have an accessible English equivalent.

## Exit tasks and observable rubric

1. Give a two-minute account of an ordinary outing: when, where, what happened
   and what changed; correctly express four of five elicited event relationships.
2. Negotiate one of two travel or purchase options in six turns; state a reason,
   a comparison and one permission/ability constraint without a model sentence.
3. Read a short schedule plus three messages; recover at least 80% of key facts.
   Write 80–120 Chinese characters about a routine and one past event, with
   English supplied in the tutor's feedback rather than the assessment prompt.

Score **task completion**, **comprehension**, **lexical/grammatical control**,
**spoken/written intelligibility**, **independent interaction and repair**
from 0–4. A 4 meets all task conditions independently; 3 meets most with at most
one clarification and nonblocking errors; 2 completes about half with repeated
support; 1 relies on isolated memorized fragments; 0 supplies no relevant evidence.
Suggested exit: 15/20, no dimension below 2, repeated on a new topic.

Ensure every row has an introduction record. Sample 80 new and 20 earlier IDs
across modules: target 85% recognition and 70% contextual production; failed
aspects, tones or sense distinctions receive a separate remediation plan.
These thresholds and task lengths are local teaching choices, not official
exam thresholds, syllabi or guaranteed completion times.
