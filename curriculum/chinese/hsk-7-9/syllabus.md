# Shared advanced bands 7–9: precision, synthesis and mediation

**Basis:** the advanced grouping in GF0025-2021, not legacy HSK 2.0 or a
2025/2026 exam syllabus transcription. This folder adds 5,606 normalized
headwords (10,969 across the collection), 35 original teaching constructs
`zh-hsk7-9-gNNN`, and 70 original bilingual examples.

**There is one shared lexical inventory.** Band 7, 8 and 9 below are authored
teaching challenge profiles, not invented separate official word lists or
certified assessment descriptors. No official score boundaries are supplied.

## Prerequisites and communicative outcomes

Demonstrate band-6 synthesis, discourse control, cautious inference and accurate
source attribution. Diagnose specialist knowledge separately from language
ability. Establish modern formal prose competence before compact literary
constructions; being able to recognize a literary form does not make it
appropriate in ordinary conversation.

Develop extended academic and professional discussion, critical reading,
genre-sensitive writing, faithful English/Chinese mediation, argument revision
and negotiation of subtle ambiguity. A large lexicon alone cannot establish
these capabilities.

## Ordered recurring advanced modules

Introduce around 12–20 new entries per session only when sustained review
supports them. At this scale, the inventory requires many cycles and hundreds
of sessions, not a short course. Use vocabulary topics, English-gloss searches and
an explicit remaining-ID queue. Revisit all lower-band prerequisites.

Use multiple content domains within every cycle: public policy, education,
science and technology, culture, organizational decisions, environment and
everyday professional life. Do not presume expertise in each domain; supply a
bilingual factual brief before evaluating language.

Every remaining unseen ID must be assigned to a later cycle, including
general/abstract and specialized dictionary entries. Heuristic topics are
discovery aids, not complete curriculum categories. Avoid requiring productive
use of every archaic or regional sense; explicitly record a reading-only target
where appropriate. Each headword must still have a reviewed core-sense target.

## Band differentiation without fabricated word splits

| Teaching profile | Input and support | Expected performance |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Familiar academic/professional topics; glossary of necessary domain terms; two clear sources | Give an organized synthesis, separate evidence from opinion, sustain a reasoned discussion and translate key propositions faithfully. |
| 8 | Less familiar topics; three contrasting sources; limited planning and indirect stance | Reconcile disagreement, handle implicit qualifications, adapt a text for a different audience and defend a nuanced interpretation. |
| 9 | Dense cross-domain material; competing interpretations and registers; minimal support | Mediate precise meanings and ambiguity, evaluate rhetorical framing, produce polished genre-appropriate prose and revise strategically under challenge. |

Greater sophistication means greater control, not gratuitously inserting rare
words or literary patterns. Receptive recognition of compact forms can precede
productive use. Assess each skill independently; a strong translator may still
need speaking work.

## Practice, review and tutor workflow

1. Select an evidence-bounded problem and supply original or licensed bilingual
   source passages. Do not scrape a proprietary textbook or fabricate citations.
2. Elicit a summary before interpretation; mark who asserts each proposition.
3. Compare close lexical choices, rhetorical certainty, necessary/sufficient
   conditions, and a literal versus natural translation.
4. Debate or mediate an information gap, then rewrite the argument for a new
   audience. Ask for one justified omission and one necessary qualification.
5. Give precise bilingual feedback and a corrected retry; preserve any deliberate
   ambiguity rather than silently resolving it.

Review at one, three, seven, fourteen, thirty and ninety days; shorten intervals
after failures. Interleave formal/conversational pairs and new/earlier bands.
Keep a collocation and register error log alongside word-ID mastery. Use real
audio evidence for speech/listening; supplied files themselves contain no audio.
Every tutor-produced Chinese passage, phrase or example requires an English
equivalent. Explanations of a difficult word must not introduce an untranslated
chain of additional difficult words.

## Exit tasks and observable rubrics

Use original or authorized unseen prompts and matched English references.
Topic knowledge should not substitute for language performance.

- **Profile 7:** synthesize two 800–1,200-character texts into a six-minute
  briefing; write 800–1,000 characters; translate a short practical/analytical
  paragraph, preserving all material facts and qualifications.
- **Profile 8:** compare three sources totaling 2,000–3,000 characters, identify
  conflicting assumptions, conduct an eight-minute defended discussion, write
  1,000–1,400 characters and adapt the summary for a nonspecialist audience.
- **Profile 9:** critically synthesize a 3,000–4,000-character mixed-genre packet,
  sustain ten minutes of challenged discussion, produce 1,500–2,000 polished
  characters and explain two legitimate translation choices without adding facts.

These lengths and tasks are authored teaching challenges, not official exam
requirements. Accessibility or writing-mode adjustments must be recorded.

Score each dimension 0–4: **task/genre organization**, **comprehension and source
fidelity**, **lexical/grammatical precision**, **register and spoken/written
intelligibility**, **independent interaction/mediation/revision**:

- **4:** fulfills the profile independently; preserves all central propositions,
  stance and qualifications; errors are rare and nonmaterial.
- **3:** fulfills its main communicative demands with a minor omission or slip;
  can repair it after a clarification rather than a supplied answer.
- **2:** loses a central implication, source boundary or register requirement,
  or repeatedly needs substantive support.
- **1:** produces largely derivative fragments or repeatedly distorts meaning.
- **0:** no relevant assessable evidence.

Suggested local progression: profile 7 at 17/20, profile 8 at 18/20, profile 9
at 19/20, with every dimension at least 3, on two different topics. Any fabricated
source, polarity reversal or uncorrected material translation distortion must
be remediated before progression regardless of the numerical total. These
thresholds confer no official HSK band and are not a substitute for independent
expert assessment.

For the vocabulary audit, verify introduction of all 5,606 new headwords across
the advanced course, then sample 200 advanced plus 50 earlier IDs across topics
and sense types: aim for 90% receptive recognition and 80% appropriate
production of designated productive senses. A 250-item sample estimates
performance; it does not prove mastery of every word. Track fragile and
reading-only items transparently and continue maintenance after course exit.
