How to play & prototype word deck

Words travel right to left on desktop and fall toward the bottom shield on mobile. Match any incoming word; you do not need to select a target first. If the same word appears twice, your answer clears the one nearest the shield.

Every 20 seconds a new wave increases the spawn rate and the speed of new words. Up to eight words can be on screen. Wrong answers break your streak; missed words cost a shield. Pause anytime with the pause button or Escape. Switching tabs pauses automatically.

Typing accepts the displayed translation and listed English alternatives, ignoring case, extra spaces, and final sentence punctuation. Chinese answers require the actual characters; use your Chinese keyboard/IME. Pinyin answers accept tone marks (shuǐ) or tone numbers (shui3); the tones must match. Spaces between syllables are optional; neutral tones can be omitted or written 0 or 5.

Pinyin annotations are optional for Chinese ↔ English. They appear only over Chinese words, not English answers. Pronunciation-matching modes always hide annotations to avoid giving away the answer.

Standalone experiment using a small sample deck—not your app knowledge set. No saved scores, microphone, AI calls, or changes to learning progress. Spoken translations are planned for a later version.
