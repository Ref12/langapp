# Original Korean monoline pilot: CC0 1.0

Original coordinates, contextual forms, composite-letter and layout recipes,
the two punctuation candidates, and their generated paths are offered under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** to the extent rights apply:
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.

Authorship: original langapp contribution, **AI-assisted**, 2026-09-15.
The creator relayed user approval to create an original template pilot with
review before scaling. This authorizes candidate authoring, **not professional
Korean handwriting certification or blanket approval of the results**.

The source files are `characters/recipes/original-jamo.yaml` and
`characters/recipes/original-layouts.yaml`. Every emitted variant pins both
recipe files and this asset-specific notice through the manifest. The
Korean-only composer places explicitly named original forms using uniform
scale and translation. It does not fit individual glyph bounding boxes.

No geometry is traced from UnPen, Inter, ordinary fonts, external SVG outlines,
annotation arrows, unlicensed neural models, or private handwriting recordings.
The separately retained stroke-diagram README/license are source-gap evidence,
**not the provenance or license of these original paths**.

Recipe revision 2 corrects defects identified during an AI-assisted comparison
with public King Sejong Institute Foundation teaching diagrams and user
feedback. Only original comparison notes and source citations are retained;
no textbook page artwork, font outlines, or extracted coordinates are included.
This comparison does not convert the candidates into qualified-reviewed assets.

All generated variants are **unreviewed**. Their metadata distinguishes
mechanical validation from visual and qualified Korean-language review.
Original stroke grouping, direction, spacing, and fixed-weight density remain
candidate decisions requiring review. An absence of mechanical warnings is not
approval. Some compound/tense/cluster forms are intentionally flagged for
further contextual design rather than hidden by thinner pens or fake paths.

The bounded pilot consists of 118 exact modern-jamo foundation representations,
54 explicitly listed curriculum syllables, and two Common-script literal
signs, FULL STOP and QUESTION MARK. The latter are not Hangul. A short,
nonzero path approximates the dot's pen tap; it is not a zero-length placeholder.
Remaining curriculum syllables stay explicitly blocked. Neither the two
phonetic-display support glyphs nor any pronunciation metadata substitutes for
standard vocabulary spelling or fills absent source readings.

Release and bulk generation remain gated on review of the representatives,
every implemented contextual family, unexercised context cases, and flagged
exceptions. No recognition model, tracing score, pronunciation assessment,
mastery engine, or production writing UI is included.

There is no warranty of Korean handwriting correctness, suitability for
teaching, fitness for a particular purpose, or noninfringement. Existing
dictionary and reference-text licenses remain separate and unchanged.
